Teaching Workspace

Transformers & Attention

A self-paced course on how an LLM actually processes context.

Your mission: hold an accurate, mechanistic model of attention — and connect it back to your day-to-day work with LLMs and AI agents.

Lessons

Lesson 1
The Attention Blend
What softmax does — turning Q·K scores into a weighted mix of values.
Lesson 2
The Host's Evening
The whole mechanism as one story — a token, a longing, and a wise Host who shows it what matters. Layers, the causal mask, feed-forward, and the blend, felt from the inside.
Lesson 3
The Ledger of You
What the token actually is: one list of numbers, written into by every room. The residual stream as additive store, cancellable signal, and shared scratchpad — and the feed-forward Study opened all the way up.
Lesson 4
The Reading of the Last Line
How layers and a summit of numbers become an actual next word: the layer ladder, the last position holding the whole window, the unembedding weigh-against-the-dictionary, temperature, and the same door read backwards. The capstone of the forward pass.
Lesson 5 · side-lesson
The Pinned Note
Why chain-of-thought works: the past is frozen (causal mask), so emitting an intermediate result as a token moves it from the finite internal scratchpad into the durable token stream — buying compute and memory. Applied to your agent work.
Lesson 6
The Host's Stewards
Multi-head attention: the Host is a council of parallel readers, each attuned to a different resonance, their cards merged by a chamberlain. Includes induction heads — the mechanism behind in-context learning and why prompting works.
Lesson 7 · side-lesson
The Crowded Room
Applied: why user/system messages are the highest-leverage knob (you set the board), and why long contexts degrade — attention dilution, lost-in-the-middle, out-of-distribution length, and self-reinforcing induction-head loops.
Lesson 8 · reflection
Tokens, Meaning, and the Membrane
A philosophical coda: the LLM as a meaning-transducer. Language as lossy compression, tokens as anchors, the distributional hypothesis, and meaning as both the bridge and the contested boundary between human and machine cognition.
Lesson 9
The Ten Thousand Evenings
Training: the Host's apprenticeship. Random init → guess/surprise/blame/nudge → meaning sediments out. Plus assembling the diet (corpus, weighting, shuffling, packing) and finishing school (fine-tuning/RLHF). Why the model you use is already frozen.

Rooms still ahead: where each guest's seat comes from (positional encoding), and what a gradient really is (the math under the metaphor).
