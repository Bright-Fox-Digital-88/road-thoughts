# Established: strong prior mental model of LLM internals

The user comes to this workspace having already studied how LLMs handle attention, context,
and memory, plus higher-level philosophy about tokens/language as a barrier between human
symbols and machine "meaning." This is disclosed prior knowledge, not yet-tested here.

Why it matters for future sessions:
- The zone of proximal development is unusually high on the *machine* side. Teach the human
  brain by contrast/parallel with LLM internals — it's the fastest scaffold and the user
  actively wants the comparison.
- But the analogy is a double-edged tool: the user's LLM intuitions will actively mislead on
  scarcity (soft weighting vs. hard bottleneck), statefulness, and bottom-up interrupts.
  Lessons should name where the parallel breaks, explicitly, not just where it holds.
- Mission is comparative by design: the goal is to reason well about human–AI hand-off, so
  every human-brain concept should eventually land back on that collaboration workflow.
