Transformers & Attention · Lesson 4 of N

The Reading of the Last Line

How a stack of layers and a summit of numbers become an actual next word — with no spark, just a dot product and a dice roll.

The one idea: The whole context funnels up through the layers into a single summit vector — the last token. To predict the next word, that one vector is weighed against every word in a fixed dictionary. The layers did the hard part by positioning the summit; the read-out is trivial.

This lesson closes the loop opened in Lesson 3: a token ends its journey as a prediction. Here is exactly how that prediction is taken — and why it feels like lightning but isn't.

Movement I · The ascending movements
🪜 The Ascending Movements the layer hierarchy (emergent, not assigned) The movements of the evening progress in rough character — but no one assigned the progression. Engineers design only the scaffold (how many layers, how wide) and the single goal (predict the next token). The division of labour self-organises to serve that goal. The rough, fuzzy, overlapping ladder researchers observe: form → structure → meaning → prediction.
  • Lowest rungs — "what is this, literally?" Token identity, morphology, part of speech. Surface form.
  • Lower-middle — "how do these attach?" Syntax: phrases, what modifies what, agreement.
  • Middle — "who did what to whom?" Semantic roles, coreference (resolving it), entity tracking.
  • Upper-middle — "what does this mean, in context?" Broad semantics, recalled facts, tone, intent.
  • Top — "so what comes next?" Abstract, prediction-shaped. The representation turns from what I am toward what follows.
Design lives at the level of structure and objective; the ladder itself is grown by training, not authored. And it's a smear, not a staircase — information is sometimes built and later discarded.
Movement II · The summit holds the whole window
🏔️ The Summit the final position already contains the context Say 500 tokens fill the window. To predict the next word the model uses one vector — the last token's — not all 500, and not a fresh sum over them. The paradox dissolves once you see when the aggregation happened: at every layer, the last position reached back over all 500 and pulled their blend into itself. The whole window funnelled upward into the summit. By read-out time there is nothing left to aggregate — the single summit vector is the distilled context.

500 contextualised tokens → (attention funnels them up, layer by layer) → 1 summit vector → read out

Why the last position? It's the only one allowed to see all 500 (the causal mask puts everything to its left). It is the most-informed point in the sequence — the natural place to ask "what next?"
Movement III · The weighing against the dictionary
📖 The Dictionary of Signatures the unembedding matrix A table of ~50,000 fixed signatures — one per possible token, each a list of numbers the same width as the summit. Frozen after training; identical regardless of context. The summit is the one thing that changes; the dictionary is the constant it's read against.
⚖️ The Weighing logits → softmax → probabilities For each dictionary word, take one dot product against the summit: score(word) = summit · signature(word). The same resonance trick as attention — alignment as relevance — now against the whole dictionary instead of the guests in the room. "Dog food" gets exactly one number. Fifty thousand scores (logits) pass through softmax and become a probability over every possible next word.

So the comparison is one context-soaked vector against 50,000 fixed signatures — not 50,000 words against 500 tokens. The 500 already did their work; they live inside the one. The layers' whole effort was to position the summit so that its nearest dictionary signatures are the plausible next words. The read-out is trivial; the genius was the positioning.

Movement IV · How boldly to choose
🌡️ The Boldness Dial temperature & sampling Before softmax, the scores can be scaled. Low temperature sharpens toward the single likeliest word (confident, near-deterministic; at zero, just take the top one). High temperature flattens the spread, handing probability to rarer words ("creative"). Then one token is drawn from the distribution. (Cousins top-k and top-p trim the candidate list first.) This dial is the only thing deciding how boldly the model commits.
Movement V · The same door, read backwards
🔁 The Door, Read Backwards weight tying + the autoregressive loop The network is bookended by the same table. At the threshold, the embedding table turned a token into a vector (a lookup). At the summit, that same table — often literally the same weights, transposed — turns a vector back into token scores. You enter through a door and leave through the same door read backwards. Then the chosen token is appended and the whole pass runs again — predict one, append, predict the next. That loop, reusing the cached past, is generation.
Where's the lightning? There isn't any. The "animating spark" is just a dot product against the dictionary, a softmax, and a dice roll. No threshold of life is crossed — Frankenstein's monster doesn't get zapped alive; the body was assembled so precisely that measuring it against a list of words lights up the right one.
This is the whole machine you work with, end to end: token → vector → (gather, recall, accumulate) × N layers → summit → weigh against the dictionary → soften → sample → next token → repeat. Every knob you touch as a practitioner lives somewhere on this path — context length feeds the window, the prompt seeds the tokens, and temperature is the one dial at the very end deciding boldness.

The gears beneath this lesson

  • The Ascending Movements → the emergent layer hierarchy (form → structure → meaning → prediction); designed objective, grown division of labour.
  • The Summit → only the final position's vector is read out; attention already funnelled the whole window into it.
  • The Dictionary of Signatures → the (frozen) unembedding matrix, one signature per vocabulary token.
  • The Weighing → dot products give logits; softmax gives a probability over the vocabulary.
  • The Boldness Dial → temperature and sampling (top-k / top-p) choose how boldly to commit.
  • The Door, Read Backwards → weight tying (input embedding = output unembedding), and the autoregressive generate-append-repeat loop.

Rooms still ahead
