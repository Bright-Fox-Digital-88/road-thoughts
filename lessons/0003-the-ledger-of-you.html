Transformers & Attention · Lesson 3 of N

The Ledger of You

What the token actually is — one list of numbers, written into by every room, that starts as a name and ends as a guess.

The one idea: A token is not a word travelling through the network. It is a single fixed-length list of numbers — the residual stream — that every layer adds into. It is at once a store of gradual truth (the meaning you've become) and a scratchpad (working notes the rooms leave for each other). Same numbers, both jobs.

This lesson answers the question that's been lurking under the Evening: when your cards get spent and "something changes," what changes, and in what format? The answer dissolves the sense of amorphous, hidden state — there is exactly one thing being passed, and it's concrete.

Movement I · There is only one thing, and it is a list of numbers
📒 The Ledger of You the residual stream At every moment you are a single page: a fixed-length list of numbers — 768 of them in a small model, thousands in a large one. That length never changes, layer to layer. This page is the only state. There is no shadow file, no second store. Every card, every judgement, every recalled fact is computed from this page and folded back into it.

So when the Host reads you to mint new cards, what is he reading? This page. When "something changes," what changes? The numbers on this page — same 768 slots, new values:

you = [ 0.21, −1.07, 0.04, 0.88, … ]  →  [ 0.31, −0.77, −0.16, 0.95, … ]

Movement II · The Ledger is written only by addition
➕ The Rule of the Ledger the residual connection No room ever crosses out the page and rewrites it. Each room only ever adds a new line: you_after = you_before + update. Drinking the Cup is adding the blend into the page. The Study's verdict is another line added. You are never replaced — only accumulated. That is why you stay continuously you even as you deepen.
✒️ The Cancelling Line erasure via negative numbers "Erasing" sounds mystical; it isn't. Because numbers can be negative, a room can add a line that is the opposite of an earlier one, and the running total reads as if the first were struck out. Add −0.9 where a +0.9 sits, and the signal nets to zero. No one decides this in the moment — the reflex was trained into the pen over ten thousand evenings, firing automatically when its pattern appears.
Movement III · The Study, fully opened

The "mirror" was evasive. Here is what is really in that room — and it is only four moves, all performed on your page of numbers:

  1. Expand. Your ~768 numbers are unfolded into a much wider workspace — typically 4× wider, ~3,072 — so every specialist gets room to check its pattern.
  2. Fire selectively. Each of those ~3,072 units passes a simple non-linear gate. Most go dark; a few stay lit. This gate is what lets a specialist stay silent until its exact pattern shows up.
  3. Contract. The lit specialists' notes are folded back down to ~768.
  4. Add. That result is added into the Ledger (the Rule, again).
🗂️ The Three Thousand Specialists the feed-forward network as associative memory The Study's walls hold thousands of narrow experts, each trained to recognise one pattern and carry one morsel of stored knowledge. "Past-tense verb after a subject." "Second half of a famous name." "Something about Paris, the capital." You present your current self to all of them at once; the handful whose pattern matches light up and hand you their notes; the rest stay dark. This is where a huge share of the model's knowledge actually lives. Attention moved information between tokens; the Study recalls knowledge into one token, alone.
The division of labour, made sharp: Attention = communication (gather and mix across tokens). Feed-forward = recall (consult a vast private library and write what's relevant into yourself). Both end the same way: a list of numbers added into your page.
Movement IV · The Ledger is also a chalkboard
🏷️ The Margin Notes the residual stream as a shared workspace Rooms don't only record who you've become — they leave working notes for each other. An early steward scrawls "the subject tonight is plural" into a corner of the page; a later steward, needing exactly that, reads it and bends the coming verb to "are." Each component writes to certain directions and reads from others — so the page is a message bus, not just a diary. Some lines are durable truth; some are sticky-notes meant to be read once and struck out. There is only one Ledger, and it serves as diary and chalkboard at once.

This dual use is exactly why reading a model's mind is hard: durable meaning and throwaway scratch share the same finite page, packed into overlapping directions. Researchers reverse-engineering "circuits" are, in effect, working out which margins hold which scratch variables, and which rooms write and read them.

Movement V · By the last page, you are a guess
🔮 The Last Line the representation becomes a prediction Your page drifts with every room — not randomly, but toward a destination. Bottom layers: "I am the token bank." Middle: "I am bank, the river kind, subject of this clause." Top: "…and the next word is probably flooded." You begin as what you are and end as what you imply comes next. By the final page you've stopped describing yourself and become a prediction.

This is why grabbing the original token back out of a deep page is so hard. The change is one-way by burial and repurposing, not clean overwrite — so a faint echo of who-you-arrived-as lingers in the early pages (the Rule only ever added to you), while the late pages have been repurposed into prophecy. (How that final page is read out into an actual next word — the unembedding — is the next room.)

For your work: this is why "which layer" matters when you pull vectors out of a model for search or similarity. Early pages carry lexical/surface signal; middle pages carry the richest contextual meaning; the last page is contaminated with next-token prediction and is often not the best for similarity. And the finite width of the page is why chain-of-thought works — telling a model to "think step by step" hands it a bigger, external scratchpad (tokens) when its internal page runs out of room.

The gears beneath this lesson

  • The Ledger of You → the residual stream: one fixed-length vector, the only persistent state.
  • The Rule of the Ledger → the residual connection (every update is added, never overwritten).
  • The Cancelling Line → "erasure" = adding a negative-pointing vector; a trained reflex, not a runtime choice.
  • The Three Thousand Specialists → the feed-forward network: expand → fire → contract → add; an associative memory holding much of the model's knowledge.
  • The Margin Notes → the residual stream as a shared communication channel/workspace; components write and read subspaces, passing messages (composition).
  • The Last Line → the representation transforming from "the token" into a prediction of the next token.

Rooms still ahead

  • The Reading of the Last Line → the unembedding: how the final page becomes a probability over the whole vocabulary, and how one next word is chosen (this is where temperature lives).
  • The Host's stewards → multi-head attention: many readers of the room at once, each attuned to a different kind of resonance.
