Transformers & Attention · Lesson 9 of N

The Ten Thousand Evenings

How the Host learned his craft — where the meaning-space comes from, and how the diet that builds it is assembled.

The one idea: No one designs the meaning-space. It sediments out of a trillion tiny corrections to one relentless game — guess the next token, measure the surprise, assign the blame, nudge every knob. Understanding is the fossil left behind by getting very, very good at prediction.

You've met the Host only at the end of his training — wise, unhurried, already able to translate your longing into the right cards. This lesson is his origin. It answers the question under all the others: how does the meaning-space get built?

Movement I · The blank Host
🕯️ The Blank Host random initialization On his first night he knows nothing. His card-templates are filled with random ink; the coordinates on every Invitation are gibberish — "cat" and "philosophy" and "the" scattered with no rhyme between them. The stewards have no specialties; the libraries are empty shelves. The vast meaning-space is, on night one, pure noise.
Movement II · The game, and the measure of surprise
🚪 Guess Who Comes Next the next-token objective, self-supervised He is handed a real gathering — a true page torn from the world — and asked, at every moment, to guess who walks through the door next. The genius of the schooling: the door always opens and reveals the truth, so reality itself is the answer key. No tutor needed to grade him — the next token is simply there. That's why he can practise on every gathering ever recorded.
😲 The Measure of Surprise the loss (cross-entropy) Each guess earns one number: how startled was he by what actually came next? Expected the right guest with confidence — barely a flinch. Put his faith in the wrong one — a jolt. Low surprise when he assigned high probability to the truth; high surprise when he didn't. That single number is the entire report card.
Movement III · The accountant and the nudge
🧮 The Accountant Walking Backward backpropagation After each guess a meticulous accountant traces backward through every decision that led to it — every template, every shelf-note, every coordinate — and works out, for each knob in the building, a single verdict: "this choice made you more surprised; that one made you less." Perfectly fair credit and blame, apportioned to millions (billions) of knobs at once.
🎚️ The Nudge gradient descent Then every knob is turned a hair in the direction that would have left him less surprised. Not a lurch — a whisper (the step size, or "learning rate"). After one evening he is very nearly the same fool he was that morning. The correction is tiny on purpose: big jumps overshoot and thrash; small steps let structure settle.
Movement IV · Ten thousand evenings
⛰️ Structure Precipitates scale & emergence He does this not once but for ten thousand — ten trillion — moments. And like a canyon carved not by one flood but by a river that never stops, structure precipitates out of the noise. Gibberish coordinates self-organise: guests who behave alike drift to neighbouring positions, because that made him less surprised. Stewards find specialties; shelves fill with the patterns of the world — grammar, fact, the rhythm of what follows what — because knowing them was the only way to stop being startled.
He never once tried to "understand." He only ever tried to be less surprised by the next guest. Understanding was never the aim — it is the fossil left behind by ten thousand evenings of prediction. Meaning is what precipitates when you get very good at guessing what comes next. That is how the space you've explored got built: not authored, but sedimented.
Movement V · Assembling the diet

The apprentice is only as good as the evenings he studies — so enormous, unglamorous effort goes into assembling the diet before a single nudge. The raw world becomes training batches through roughly these stages:

  1. Collect & extract — gather raw material (web crawls, code, books) and pull clean text from messy HTML/PDF.
  2. Quality-filter — discard spam, gibberish, wrong-language, and unsafe content.
  3. Deduplicate — remove exact and near-duplicate documents; dupes cause memorisation and waste compute.
  4. Decontaminate — strip anything overlapping the evaluation benchmarks, so the model isn't secretly tested on what it trained on.
  5. Mix & weight — set the proportions of the recipe (below).
  6. Tokenize — run it all through the tokenizer; the corpus becomes a stream of integer token IDs.
  7. Pack — fit the stream into fixed-length training sequences (below).
  8. Shuffle — randomise document order (below).
⚖️ Weighting the Diet sampling proportions "Upweighting" a source mostly means sampling it more often — drawing code 3× more than its natural share, say. It's how much of the apprentice's diet is code-parties vs novels vs forum-brawls. Downweighting by subsetting means part of a source may be never seen at all — so it's partial exclusion, not just softer emphasis. Proportions are chosen by small- scale ablations (train little models on different mixes, keep what scores best).
🔀 The Shuffle de-biasing, not disambiguation Order within a document is sacred signal (kept); order between documents is an accident of collection (noise). Shuffling erases the accident so each batch is a fair miniature of the whole world — giving unbiased gradient steps, no recency bias or forgetting, no learning of spurious streaks, and smoother descent. The bulk is shuffled to cancel order-bias; the final "annealing" stretch deliberately re-introduces it, biasing the last nights toward the highest-quality data.
📦 Packing Without Snipping document boundaries & best-fit packing Short documents are packed together to fill a sequence, separated by an end-of-document token (and ideally masked apart so one can't attend to another). Documents longer than the window must span — and attention never crosses a sequence boundary, so those pieces are seen in isolation. To avoid needless mid-document cuts (which teach the model to fabricate continuations from nothing), smart pipelines use best-fit packing: bin-pack so a small remaining gap is filled by a document that fits, rather than snipping the top off a long one. Fewer truncations measurably help — less hallucination, better coherence.
Movement VI · Finishing school, and the freezing
🎓 Finishing School fine-tuning, instruction-tuning, RLHF After the long apprenticeship, a short, mannered coda: kind tutors teach him etiquette — how to take an instruction gracefully, when to speak as host and when to listen as a guest (the role costumes), what tone tends to please (preference-tuning / RLHF). But this shapes his manners, not his knowledge. The entire meaning-space was already sedimented in the ten thousand evenings of raw prediction. Finishing school taught him how to carry his wisdom, not how to have it.
🧊 The Frozen Host weights locked at inference Then his schooling ends and every knob is locked forever. The wise figure who took your hand at your party is an apprentice who has lived ten thousand lifetimes of evenings and will never change again — identical for every guest, every gathering. That is why, at inference, he is frozen; your conversation does not teach him. Learning happened once, in the evenings; what you get is the finished man.
This closes the biggest loop for your work. The model doesn't "learn" from your prompts — its weights are frozen; what looks like learning in-context is the induction-head trick (Lesson 6) reading the board you set, not the Host changing. Everything the model knows was fixed in training; every choice at inference is that frozen knowledge, read out. Knowing where the line sits — trained knowledge vs. in-context steering — is what lets you predict what a model can and can't do.

The gears beneath the evening

  • The Blank Host → random initialization: weights and embeddings start as noise.
  • Guess Who Comes Next → next-token prediction, self-supervised (the data is its own answer key).
  • The Measure of Surprise → the loss (cross-entropy): low when high probability was placed on the truth.
  • The Accountant → backpropagation: computing each weight's contribution to the error.
  • The Nudge → gradient descent: each weight stepped a little (the learning rate) to reduce loss.
  • Structure Precipitates → scale & emergence: meaning appears as a byproduct of prediction, never a stated goal.
  • Weighting / Shuffle / Packing → the data pipeline: sampling proportions, de-biasing order, and best-fit document packing.
  • Finishing School → fine-tuning / instruction-tuning / RLHF: shapes behaviour, not core knowledge.
  • The Frozen Host → weights locked after training; identical at every inference.
